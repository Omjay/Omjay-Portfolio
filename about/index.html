Data engineering built around measurable outcomes.

I am Om Jay Mishra, an Azure-focused Data Engineer based in Chennai, India. I design, build and improve data platforms that make large datasets faster to process, easier to govern and more dependable for analytics teams.

Professional focus

My core work spans Azure Databricks, Snowflake, PySpark, Delta Lake, Azure Data Factory, SQL and Python. I have worked across ingestion, incremental processing, medallion architecture, Gold-layer modelling, orchestration, data-quality controls, Unity Catalog, CI/CD and production support. I care about the complete delivery path: source reliability, transformation correctness, efficient compute, discoverable data and useful downstream reporting.

Evidence from my work

All three of my roles have been at startups, with small data teams: I am the only data engineer at ExaQube (about 10 people), I led a two-person data team at Azimuth GRC (about 30 people), and I was the junior member of a two-person team at PlanetSpark (about 40 people). At Azimuth GRC I built the Azure Data Factory and Databricks pipeline that replaced a manual six-hour process with a thirty-minute run. It started at 20M records and still ran in about thirty minutes past 500M+. Every Tuesday it checked about 150,000 records against 85 compliance tests, around 12.75 million results a run, into a results table that grew to about 4 billion rows. Around it I led the move to a Lakehouse, designed a Gold-layer star schema that made queries 65% faster, set up Unity Catalog governance, and cut Databricks DBU use by 25%. At ExaQube Technologies I am moving legacy production processes onto Databricks Workflows and Delta Lake, and for a client I automated branch-based Snowflake dev environments that zero-copy clone the production database for each feature branch. Earlier, at PlanetSpark, I automated reporting and engagement analysis for more than 10,000 students.
