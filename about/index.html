I fix slow, costly and manual data work.

I am Om Jay Mishra, a data engineer based in Chennai, India. I take freelance and consulting work on Azure Databricks, Snowflake and Azure, making large datasets faster to process, easier to govern and more dependable for analytics teams.

What I work with

I work mostly with Azure Databricks, Snowflake, PySpark, Delta Lake, Azure Data Factory, SQL and Python, across ingestion, incremental processing, medallion layers, Gold-layer modelling, orchestration, data-quality checks, Unity Catalog, CI/CD and production support. I care about the whole path from source to report: reliable sources, correct transformations, efficient compute and useful reporting.

Evidence from my work

All three of my roles have been at startups, with small data teams: I am the only data engineer at ExaQube (about 10 people), I led a two-person data team at Azimuth GRC (about 30 people), and I was the junior member of a two-person team at PlanetSpark (about 40 people). At Azimuth GRC I built the Azure Data Factory and Databricks pipeline that replaced a manual six-hour process with a run of about thirty minutes at 20M records. It started at 20M records and still ran in about thirty minutes past 500M+. Every Tuesday it checked about 150,000 records against 85 compliance tests, around 12.75 million results a run, into a results table that grew to about 4 billion rows. Around it I led the move to a Lakehouse, designed a Gold-layer star schema that made queries 65% faster, set up Unity Catalog governance, and cut Databricks DBU use by 25%. At ExaQube Technologies I am moving legacy production processes onto Databricks Workflows and Delta Lake, and for a client I automated branch-based Snowflake dev environments that zero-copy clone the production database for each feature branch. Earlier, at PlanetSpark, I automated reporting and engagement analysis for more than 10,000 students.
