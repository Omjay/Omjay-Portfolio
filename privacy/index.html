Privacy

A small, static portfolio with limited data collection.

This privacy notice explains what happens when you visit the Om Jay Mishra Data Engineering Portfolio. The site is published as a static website through GitHub Pages and does not provide accounts, forms, payments, personalized dashboards or an application database.

Information this site collects

The portfolio itself does not intentionally set first-party advertising cookies, create user profiles or store form submissions. Like most hosting services, GitHub Pages and the networks involved in delivering the site may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or User-Agent, requested URL, time, referral data and diagnostic logs. That infrastructure processing is controlled by the relevant provider and may be needed for security, reliability and content delivery.

External resources and links

Some pages may request fonts, technology icons or other public assets from third-party content networks. Following links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Microsoft, Databricks, Coursera or another external site takes you to that provider, whose own privacy terms apply. The portfolio cannot control how those external services process information. You can block third-party requests through browser privacy settings if you prefer.

Email

If you email the published professional address, the content you send is processed by the email providers used by both parties and retained as needed to answer the enquiry or maintain a professional record. Do not send passwords, access tokens, regulated datasets, customer records or other secrets. You may request deletion of an ordinary enquiry by replying to the same email thread, subject to legal or security retention needs.

Automated access

Search engines and AI agents may crawl public pages according to the site's robots.txt guidance. Public access does not grant permission to bypass access controls, impersonate the site owner, or use the content for unlawful purposes. Machine-readable files are provided to improve accurate retrieval and attribution.

Updates and contact

This notice may change when site functionality or hosting changes. The current published version is effective 12 September 2026. Questions about this portfolio's privacy practices can be sent to mishra.omjay@gmail.com.
